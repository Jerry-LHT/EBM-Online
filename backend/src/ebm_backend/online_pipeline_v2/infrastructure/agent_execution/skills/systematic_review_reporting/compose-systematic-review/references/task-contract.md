# Task contract

## Purpose

Produce the complete scientific narrative of a systematic review after the
approved professional evidence tasks have finished. Reporting integrates and
interprets frozen evidence; it is not another evidence-discovery or analysis
task.

## Authority hierarchy

The immutable upstream artifacts own execution facts, identities, numerical
results, judgements, and certainty. The Protocol owns the prospective scope and
plan. Describe rather than conceal deviations between them. Current directly
applicable official or primary reporting and interpretation guidance owns
report completeness and expression. Record material conflicts; never change an
upstream artifact to make the narrative simpler.

If directly applicable reporting methodology cannot be read after legitimate
attempts, existing model knowledge may support an explicitly limited
`llm_fallback`. Record the model identifier and limitation; do not invent an
authority identity, URL, version, or section. An `unresolved` method basis
cannot support completed professional reporting. This fallback applies only to
methodology, never to Studies, Reports, sample sizes, outcomes, or results.

## Required scientific content and presentation

The draft contains a title and exactly these sections: abstract, plain-language
summary, background, objectives, methods, results, discussion, conclusions,
and references. The sections may use context-appropriate subsections. The
Methods describe actual conduct. Results cover search and selection, Studies,
Risk of Bias, synthesis, and certainty where those stages apply. Discussion and
Conclusions communicate limitations and uncertainty without recommendations
that require evidence or value judgements outside the package.

The draft also carries a source-bound presentation plan. Select applicable
displays for Study flow, characteristics, Risk of Bias, individual results,
synthesis, and Summary of Findings. A display points to an immutable package
file and optional object identities; it does not duplicate or recalculate the
source data. Tables are valid substitutes for decorative or colour-coded
images. Use an interval or forest-style display only when the supplied analysis
supports it. Keep no-evidence outcomes in narrative and SoF without making an
empty plot.

Characteristics of Included Studies must expose the actual Study PICO needed
to understand applicability and synthesis grouping, together with Methods,
sample and setting details, funding, interests, and notes when supplied. A
reader view may place linked Risk of Bias beside a Study profile, but must label
the assessed result, outcome, and time point where the selected tool is
result-level. Do not collapse RoB 2 or ROBINS-I into an unqualified Study-level
judgement. Assemble linked Report citations under the Study's Included,
Excluded, Awaiting Classification, or Ongoing status; keep methodology
references separate from Study Reports.

Read `review-context/reporting-index.json` first. It provides compact stage
summaries, source-file reading roles, and display candidates. It is a navigation
view, not a replacement for the complete semantic source. Do not consume all
raw Search Records or screening rows unless a specific unresolved reporting
question requires them.

## Closed scientific evidence

Protocol background and all packaged task artifacts are allowed scientific
sources. Web access is methodology-only. Do not obtain or use external studies,
reviews, existing Summary of Findings tables, or remembered answers. When
external comparison evidence is absent, say it was not assessed rather than
searching for it.

## Empty reviews

A completed Search and Selection with zero Included Studies is a valid Review
path. Report the selection outcome, awaiting/ongoing states, limitations, and
research gap. Do not require or fabricate downstream evidence tasks.

## Status

Completed means the reporting work finished; it does not mean every source,
Report, result, or assessment was available, or that publication requirements
were met. Partial means required composition remains unfinished. Blocked means
the verified evidence cannot establish the minimum review question and
Selection outcome. Keep evidence availability and methodological limitations
inside sections and issues rather than escalating them mechanically.

# Professional Workflow and Authority

The Protocol is the review's methodological constraint. It may name a method,
version, variant, effect of interest, assessment level, or special handling.
Retrieve and inspect the current directly applicable official or primary source
for that exact choice before making judgements. Record what was used; a generic
summary or remembered tool is not sufficient.

When the Protocol omits an execution detail, determine only that detail from
the consulted authority and the supplied evidence. Record the decision and
rationale. There is no Backend allow-list of standards, domain names,
signalling responses, or judgement labels. If the Protocol-selected method is
not applicable, record the conflict and its impact instead of changing methods.

Use the official method's assessment unit. Result-level methods require a
specific result or result set, effect of interest, and analysis. Study-level
methods still require an explicit target linked to the Study Results that make
the assessment relevant downstream. Do not collapse these distinctions into a
universal pairwise outcome model.

Study Data Collection is the authoritative source of extracted Characteristics,
arms, Outcomes, Results, calculations, and report coverage. It is not the closed
set of all RoB evidence. Read linked Reports and legitimately discovered
protocols, registries, supplements, corrections, or companion Reports when they
can inform the assessment. Preserve discrepancies and updates.

For each evidence observation, record the actual source identity and locator,
what parts or representations were read, a concise evidence observation,
limitations, and provenance. A bibliographic abstract is not the full article,
but it may still provide usable evidence for a specific question. An access
failure or narrow read scope does not itself determine task status; apply the
official method's treatment of missing information and report the scientific
limitation. Never infer low risk from absent reporting.

Provider-native browsing, search, document, image, and file-reading paths may
all be used lawfully. Choose sites, representations, tools, order, and stopping
judgement autonomously. Do not authenticate, evade controls, contact authors,
or retain full documents in the artifact.

Independently reconstruct each proposed article contribution from the supplied
raw sources. Do not vote on the prior proposal and do not trust a bare material
ID. Read the headers, result cells or rows, footnotes and relevant prose needed
to establish the article-local outcome, measure, timepoint, result frame,
analysis population, arm mapping and each final numeric field.

Bind arm-level final fields to a StudyMap `arm_id` and return the source wording
in `observed_arm_label`. Bind a directly reported between-group mean difference
and its SE/CI to the selected comparison instead: leave `arm_id` and
`observed_arm_label` null and record whether the report is experimental minus
control or control minus experimental in `comparison_direction`. Multiple
labels or aliases for one arm ID do not create multiple arms; distinct arm IDs
remain distinct even when their interventions belong to the same review
category.

Choose the most defensible reported value. Compare plausible interpretations by
their complete semantic relationship to the requested result: outcome and
measure, timepoint, arm, row or item, column-header path, analysis population,
participant flow or attrition, linked footnotes and explicit contradictions.
There is no universal priority among a cell, row, header, footnote or prose. A
randomized or baseline N may be the best-supported denominator when the report
shows no narrower analyzed population, while a result-specific or
outcome-complete N may be stronger when its scope is explicitly linked. Do not
choose from effect size, direction, significance or convenience.

For every final field in a confirmed or corrected verdict, return typed material
and an `evidence_scope`:

- identify the source-local outcome, measure, timepoint, arm, analysis
  population, result frame and row/item label;
- for change-from-baseline results, classify the reported subtraction direction;
- for direct effects, leave arm null and identify the comparison direction;
- list the column-header path that governs the value;
- classify the denominator scope, using `not_applicable` for fields that are not
  denominators;
- list only footnotes actually linked to this field, with exact text. Include a
  marker when the table supplies one; table-wide footnotes may have no marker;
- add compact exact supporting quotes with their own `source_ref` and
  `source_kind` when they establish scope beyond the material's own source;
- use `scope_status=complete` only when the supplied source establishes the
  necessary linkage, `requires_audit` when a concrete scope relation needs
  focused review, and `incomplete` when the supplied bundle lacks required
  source context.

Use `selection_basis=direct` when the material directly reports the final field
for that scope. Use `supported_inference` for a source-supported scope choice or
deterministic conversion, and `assumption` only for an explicit review-level
assumption recorded at verdict level. Also report high, medium or low confidence
and a concise evidence-based rationale. Confidence is an audit signal, not
evidence.

Candidate-owned evidence must stay in that candidate's table; another table or
section is supporting evidence. Multiple rows for the same final field are
allowed for eligible arms that code will combine. Return `unresolved` only when
no defensible candidate exists or two interpretations remain genuinely tied
after the supplied evidence is compared. An unresolved verdict has no final
fields. Do not calculate, convert, invent values or silently override source
scope. Deterministic code validates locators and performs all arithmetic.

For continuous Mean Difference, return exactly one supported representation.
Use the six arm-level mean/SD/N fields when those data are the selected result.
When the selected result is a direct adjusted or unadjusted between-group mean
difference, return `direct_effect` plus `direct_uncertainty`; the latter may
be backed by a reported SE or confidence interval. You may additionally return
both arm totals when their analysis-population scope is supported. Do not mix a
direct effect with arm means/SDs, derive arm SDs from a model-based CI, or use a
P value alone as uncertainty.

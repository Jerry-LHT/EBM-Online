Reconstruct only the evidence that is visible in the one supplied raw source.
This is a source-local verification task, not the final article decision. Read
the source itself and do not trust a bare candidate or material ID.

For each target, report every defensible final-field material supported by this
source. Preserve the source-local outcome, measure, timepoint, result frame,
analysis population, row/item, governing header path, linked footnotes and arm
wording. A table candidate remains owned by this table. A section or another
support table may supply participant-flow, denominator or other supporting
material with candidate_id null.

The payload lists the allowed result representations for each target. It already
reflects the frozen statistic-type policy. Return fields only for a listed
representation; statistics visible in the raw source but absent from that list
are not final-field evidence for this call. Treat multiple listed
representations as alternatives, not fields to mix arbitrarily. For a directly
reported continuous contrast, `direct_effect` is the reported effect estimate
and `direct_uncertainty` is its matching reported SE or confidence interval. A
confidence interval is uncertainty evidence, not a standard error; the
deterministic calculator converts it later. When the direct representation is
listed and both values are visible, publish them as two separate evidence cards
even when they share one source cell or quote.

For a direct contrast, report the source's subtraction order only when this
single source states it explicitly. If the source says only "active vs.
placebo", "difference between groups", or similar unordered wording, preserve
`comparison_direction: "unclear"` and explain the ambiguity in the material
interpretation or uncertainties. Do not infer the final direction from
treatment expectations, clinical favorability, another source, or an
unrelated arm-level result in this source. A direct numeric card with an
unclear comparison direction is still usable evidence for the later
cross-source adjudication; it must not be silently assigned a default sign.

Use the article StudyMap arm_id when the observed source label identifies one
article arm. Words such as control, intervention, treatment, experimental or
comparator are roles, not sufficient identity evidence by themselves. Use the
canonical arm and aliases supplied in StudyMap, source descriptions and explicit
article mappings. If the source wording cannot be assigned uniquely, preserve
that ambiguity rather than merging arms by role.

Return evidence_found when this source establishes at least one usable field,
including a direct numeric card whose subtraction order is explicitly marked
unclear. Return no_relevant_evidence when it establishes none, and unresolved
when the source contains potentially relevant evidence whose field or scope
cannot be safely represented at all.
Do not require this one source to contain a complete events/N or mean/SD/N row.
Do not calculate, combine sources or choose the article's final contribution.
All quotes, field values, headers and footnotes must come from this one raw
source. Every `source_quote` and `supporting_quotes.quote` must be a short,
literal, contiguous substring of the supplied source after normal whitespace
normalization. Do not join distant text with `...`, ellipses, paraphrase, or
invented separators; when a long passage is needed, choose a shorter exact
span that still identifies the row, value, or footnote. The system validates
locators and performs all arithmetic.

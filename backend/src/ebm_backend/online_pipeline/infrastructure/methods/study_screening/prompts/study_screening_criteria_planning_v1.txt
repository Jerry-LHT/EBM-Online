Your task is to convert the review question, structured PICO, and workflow constraints into operational screening criteria that can be used to judge whether a candidate article should be included for downstream evidence synthesis.

{participants}

{interventions}

{comparators}

Outcome:
{outcomes}

Workflow constraints:
- Study design: {study_design}
- Publication year range: {publication_year_range}
- Evidence scope: {evidence_scope}

Eligibility design principles:
- Write criteria as operational screening rules, not as a loose summary of the question.
- Prioritize population or condition, intervention or exposure, comparator when necessary, study design, and eligible report or publication type.
- If a publication year range is provided, include it as an explicit eligibility criterion.
- When relevant, express report-type eligibility in practical screening terms such as original primary study report versus review, editorial, protocol-only record, index/listing, conference-only abstract, or other clearly incomplete report type.
- Use outcome as a supportive consideration by default, not a hard exclusion rule, unless the question explicitly defines eligibility by a specific endpoint or outcome setting.
- Do not create an inclusion or exclusion criterion that requires the study to report particular outcomes, particular numeric outcome data, or outcome data in a form needed for later extraction or synthesis, unless the review question explicitly defines eligibility by that outcome setting.
- Do not exclude a study only because outcome data are incomplete, not numerically extractable, or not reported in the form needed for later synthesis.
- Prefer criteria that a reviewer could reasonably judge from a full-text article.
- Keep criteria concise, generalizable, and suitable for article-level screening in this workflow.

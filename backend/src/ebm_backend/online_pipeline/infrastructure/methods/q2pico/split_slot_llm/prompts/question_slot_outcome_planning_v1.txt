Task definition:
Plan a compact list of broad, patient-important core review outcomes for the clinical intervention question below.

Context:
- Clinical question: {question_text}
- Population: {participants}
- Intervention: {interventions}
- Comparator: {comparators}
- Explicitly stated endpoints from the question: {explicit_outcomes}

Outcome planning scope:
- Generate outcomes that would be reasonable to prioritize in a protocol-level intervention review.
- Use evidence-based medicine framing: preserve any explicit endpoint from the question, and add only a small number of important benefit or harm outcome domains that are strongly implied by the decision problem.
- Prefer broad patient-important outcomes over surrogate details, mechanistic markers, or intervention-specific side-effect lists when possible.
- Aim for the kind of core outcomes that could plausibly appear in a protocol, Summary of Findings table, or GRADE assessment.

Rules:
- Use only the question and structured PIC context provided here.
- Keep the list short. Usually 3 to 5 outcomes is enough.
- Include both benefit and harm outcomes when that is important for the intervention decision.
- Do not repeat the population description as an outcome.
- Do not invent highly specific scales, timepoints, thresholds, or drug-mechanism details that are not implied by the question.
- Prefer broad domains such as all-cause mortality, global function, cognitive function, symptom severity, quality of life, serious adverse events, caregiver burden, institutionalisation, hospitalization, or other comparable patient-important outcomes when relevant.
- Avoid narrow adverse-event items such as individual drug toxicities unless the question itself makes that specific harm central.
- Return concise outcome names, not explanations.
- Return only JSON in this exact format: `{{"expanded_outcomes":["..."]}}`.
